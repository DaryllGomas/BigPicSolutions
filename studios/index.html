Why we build

The end of software as you knew it.
The beginning of software built for you.

For decades, software was something you were handed: built by someone else, for everyone at once, on their terms. That's changing. One person working with AI can now make the games, worlds and tools that used to take a whole studio. We build ours in the open, and we can build yours.
